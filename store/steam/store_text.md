## 关于这款游戏（简体中文）

```
[h2]天时 · 地利 · 人和[/h2]
你是董事长，手里只有一面罗盘。

《商道玄机·大亨罗盘》是一款以奇门遁甲为框架的经营策略游戏。每个月，你要读懂大运与节气带来的「天时」，调理门店选址与总部风水的「地利」，安排高管所落的八门、化解他们之间的冲突，求得「人和」。三者相乘，就是公司这个月的营收。

[h2]三个案例，三种算法[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]案例一【空间算法】新中式烘焙的跨省选址之争[/b]——30 家门店首次跨省扩张，进军郑州合肥，还是成都西安？
[*][b]案例二【时间算法】AI 教育初创公司的融资择时生死战[/b]——现金只够撑 4.7 个月，对赌融资签不签、何时签？
[*][b]案例三【人物算法】豪华创业四人组的权力归位[/b]——四位联创势成水火，内耗高达 45%，如何让团队归位？
[/list]

[h2]玩法特色[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]开局决断与董事会议题[/b]：关键月份出现紧急议题，有的选择当月见效，有的数月后才显现后果。
[*][b]每月盈亏逐项解读[/b]：结账后说明天时、地利、人和各贡献了多少，并预告下个月的天时走向。
[*][b]企业总部风水工位[/b]：在等距视角的办公室里摆放屏风、招财树、鱼缸，破解穿堂煞、背门而坐等格局，风水评分影响全部门店营收。
[*][b]高管与八门[/b]：每位高管落在不同的门，相生则互补，相克则内耗。
[*][b]随机事件[/b]：螣蛇暗流、白虎猖狂、贵人辅弼……天有不测风云。
[/list]

[i]游戏中的奇门遁甲与风水内容为游戏化的设定，仅供娱乐，不构成任何投资或经营建议。[/i]
```

## About This Game (English)

```
[h2]Heaven's Timing · Earthly Advantage · Human Harmony[/h2]
You are the chairman, and all you have is a compass.

Dynasty Tycoon is a business strategy game built on Qimen Dunjia, the ancient Chinese art of divination. Each month you read the timing of the grand cycle and the solar terms, shape your terrain through store locations and the feng shui of your headquarters, and seat your executives in the Eight Gates to keep your people in harmony. Multiply the three together and you get this month's revenue.

[h2]Three cases, three disciplines[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Case 1 · Space[/b]: a new-Chinese-style bakery chain makes its first leap across provinces. Cheap rent in Zhengzhou and Hefei, or the cultural hubs of Chengdu and Xi'an?
[*][b]Case 2 · Time[/b]: an AI-education startup with 4.7 months of runway. Sign the harsh term sheet now, or bet on a better valuation later?
[*][b]Case 3 · People[/b]: four star co-founders at each other's throats with 45% internal friction. Can you reassign the team and ship the product?
[/list]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Opening calls and board crises[/b]: some choices pay off immediately; others come back to haunt you months later.
[*][b]Every month explained[/b]: see exactly how much timing, terrain and people contributed, plus a forecast for next month.
[*][b]Feng shui headquarters[/b]: arrange screens, money trees and aquariums in an isometric office to break harmful layouts and boost revenue at every store.
[*][b]Executives and the Eight Gates[/b]: compatible gates complement each other; clashing gates breed friction.
[*][b]Random events[/b]: hidden serpents, raging white tigers and helpful patrons.
[/list]

[b]Language: Simplified Chinese only.[/b]

[i]The Qimen Dunjia and feng shui systems are gameplay mechanics for entertainment only and are not investment or business advice.[/i]
```
